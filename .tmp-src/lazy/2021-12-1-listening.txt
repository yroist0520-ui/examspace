2 Correct their mistake as soon as possible. · Give the two pets a physical checkup. · Hire a charter jet to bring the pets back. · Send another plane to continue the flight.

Section B · 第 1 篇 长对话 · 8–11 题
8 The number of ducks has declined sharply in recent years. · Climate change has little effect on the lives of wild ducks. · Duck meat is not eaten in Australia, Canada and the U.S. · Duck hunting remains legal in many parts of the world.

9 Droughts. · Bushfires. · Farming. · Hunting.

10 They are not easy to domesticate. · Their meat is not that popular. · It is not environmentally friendly. · It is not considered cost-effective.

11 They hunted ducks as a traditional sport. · They killed wild ducks and geese for food. · They raised ducks and geese for their eggs. · They poisoned wild ducks in large numbers.

Section B · 第 2 篇 长对话 · 12–15 题
12 Have her house repainted. · Replace some of her old furniture. · Move into a newly-painted house. · Calculate the cost of the paint job.

13 How long the work will take. · How much the work will cost. · How the paint job is to be done. · How many workers are needed.

14 Cover up her furniture. · Ask some friends for help. · Stay somewhere else. · Oversee the work herself.

15 She could have asked a friend for help with the paint job. · Painting a house involves more trouble than she thought. · She should have repainted her house much earlier. · Moving her furniture is harder than the paint job.

17 Discuss their academic achievements with them. · Create an ideal study environment for them. · Allow them to learn independently. · Check their homework promptly.

18 Finish them before they get tired. · Tackle the most difficult task first. · Start with something they enjoy. · Focus on the most important ones.

Section C · 第 2 篇 听力篇章 · 19–21 题

20 Impractical. · Inconsistent. · Unmanageable. · Unfair.

21 Offer them much fatter bonuses. · Improve working environment. · Encourage healthy behaviors. · Provide free lunch and snacks.

Section C · 第 3 篇 听力篇章 · 22–25 题
