Directions: Suppose the student union of your university is organizing an online discussion on interpersonal relationships. You are to write an essay on ways to maintain a warm and friendly relationship with your classmates and on the benefits of such a relationship. You will have 30 minutes for the task. You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

Firstly, active listening and empathy are key; by understanding classmates' perspectives, we can build trust and mutual respect. Secondly, collaboration in group projects can strengthen bonds, as it encourages teamwork and shared success. Moreover, participating in social events outside the classroom can create a sense of camaraderie and belonging. The benefits of nurturing such relationships are manifold. In conclusion, investing time and effort in maintaining warm and friendly relationships with classmates is essential for a fulfilling university life and future success.
